This dataset was selected during term, relaying some findings using the dataset produced 
as part of my 2c, which I am using this project to follow on from. 

Personification was found to be influenced by an array of language features, as well as experience
 - this Data Science project will explore this further within the conversations themselves, 
 to measures beyond personification, and more to general effects, use and preference.

This should hopefully inform analysis of the design choices of AI, and their consequences, as well
as perhaps offering insight into human social preference and behaviour more broadly.
